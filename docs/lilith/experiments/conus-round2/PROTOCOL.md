# Lilith CONUS research round 2 — fixed experiment protocol

Revised 2026-10-14 America/Chicago after explicit user steering to CONUS, before fitting any round-2 model or viewing audit skill. The earlier global preparation is retained as unused research; this CONUS protocol supersedes its station selection.

Scope: observed NOAA GHCN-Daily TMAX/TMIN/PRCP, 30 input reporting-days, two-day observation embargo, 14 target days; CONUS ASOS/AWOS registry cohort, not an atmospheric/severe-event forecast.

Training 1980–2016; validation/model selection 2017–2020; separate residual-interval calibration 2021–2023; previously inspected development years 2024–2025; new retrospective audit 2026-01-01 through 2026-09-30. Entire input/gap/target windows must stay in each partition. Targets stride 14 days without within-station overlap. Final NOAA revisions are retrospective, not proved available-at-issue inputs; the two-day embargo is an assumption to stress-test, not an operational availability guarantee.

Include all current NOAA HOMR ASOS/AWOS sites in the contiguous 48 states and DC that have an explicit/catalog-identity GHCN daily crosswalk within 10 km. Keep unmatched sites in the hourly acquisition backlog; do not label them missing weather. No random station cap. Current network identity does not imply pre-1991 history was measured with ASOS hardware. Allow partial variables and windows; retain observation masks and raw source flags. Seasonal normals use 1980–2016 only, correct leap-aligned month/day bins, and each input day's own normal. Held-out neural-fit stations may use their own pre-2017 climatology: this is location transfer with historical local normals, not zero-history or contiguous-region transfer.

Fixed candidates: corrected multi-variable anomaly ridge (alpha 1,100,10000, chosen per-variable on validation), a small residual MLP and a small residual Transformer, both anchored to fitted ridge outputs; one recorded seed; at most 20 epochs and 1,200 seconds each. A validation-selected 0/.25/.5/.75/1 blend against ridge is reported separately from raw neural output; wet-day probability blend selected separately by validation Brier score. Champion chosen by validation per variable only. All candidates remain research_not_promoted.

Training-only missing-input imputation: zero anomaly plus explicit masks; never fill target truth. Exclude unsupported variables with no adequate historical normal, retain partial targets and denominators. Report exact exclusions, geography and per-variable coverage. Cap training at 120,000 cases using seeded, station-balanced selection if necessary, recording sampled indices.

Score measured actual values on identical masks: RMSE/MAE/bias, same-case climatology/persistence/ridge skill, per-lead and station/latitude/season diagnostics, physical violations. Wet-day >=1mm Brier/reliability/false-alarm diagnostics do not constitute a severe-event model. Fit 90% split-conformal absolute residual intervals only on calibration years; report empirical audit coverage/width without asserting exchangeability. Estimate paired score-difference uncertainty with station/month cluster resampling and disclose limited audit duration.

No test/audit selection, thresholds, architecture or blend changes after audit scoring. Preserve failures, all candidate reports/checkpoints/prediction arrays, source/data/code hashes and Kaggle logs on a new NAS archive. Compare round-1 absolute scores only on truly matched cases; otherwise label cohorts incomparable. No automatic production promotion or real alerts.
